What

Programmable World Model is an explicit-state-driven framework: it decouples world-state maintenance and evolution from visual observation generation. Inside the loop, a state executor maintains the world state and advances it under player actions and world rules; a deterministic compiler projects the state, represented as state-augmented 3D OBBs, into pixel-aligned controls; a video model renders the observation; and a VLM-based agent writes the executable world specification from a reference image and open-ended language.

Why

Video world models are powerful interactive renderers, but plausible observations alone are not a world engine. Their state lives implicitly in visual histories, difficult to inspect, edit, or verify; controls stop at cameras and prompts without reaching entity-level state; and the world must persist beyond the current view, where off-screen entities and latent facts such as health or relations are silently forgotten. These hidden facts and rules can be expressed compactly in language, structured records, and executable code, exactly what an agent can construct and update.
